## 2. Approved Operating Contract & Volume Recognition Policy
Audited FY2025 closing leased slots: **1177**. Fractional activity is permitted.
Opening slots suffer B attrition at year start. Admit at most A new leases up to C total capacity. Surviving slots earn a full year; new leases earn half.

operating expenses (excluding D&A) and scheduled debt principal amortization are
disclosed in **EUR thousands (EUR '000)**; unit price is in **whole EUR per unit**.
