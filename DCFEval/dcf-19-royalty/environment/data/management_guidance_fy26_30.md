## 2. Approved Operating Contract & Volume Recognition Policy
Audited FY2025 closing installed units: **1191**. Fractional activity is permitted.
Add A placements at year start. First B closing units earn full unit rate, excess earns C of rate. Minimum annual bill equals 0.90 times opening units at full rate; take higher bill.

disclosed in **EUR thousands (EUR '000)**; unit price is in **whole EUR per unit**.
