## 2. Approved Operating Contract & Volume Recognition Policy
Audited FY2025 closing covered units: **882**. Fractional activity is permitted.
Expire B of opening covers; sell A new annual covers. Service revenue uses surviving opening covers plus half additions. Deduct expected credits equal to C times opening covers times 40% of unit rate.

operating expenses (excluding D&A) and scheduled debt principal amortization are
disclosed in **EUR thousands (EUR '000)**; unit price is in **whole EUR per unit**.
