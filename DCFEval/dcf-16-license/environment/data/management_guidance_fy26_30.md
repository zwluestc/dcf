## 2. Approved Operating Contract & Volume Recognition Policy
Audited FY2025 closing licenses: **1082**. Fractional activity is permitted.
Renew B of opening licenses and sign A at midyear. Recognise full-year service revenue on renewed licenses and half-year service revenue on new additions; in addition, each new addition pays an immediate upfront license fee equal to C times the full annual unit rate, recognised in full as revenue in the year signed.

while operating ratios (A/B/C) are stated directly in the contract basis. Fixed
operating expenses (excluding D&A) and scheduled debt principal amortization are
disclosed in **EUR thousands (EUR '000)**; unit price is in **whole EUR per unit**.
